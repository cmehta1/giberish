Our Philosophy

We named ourselves after nonsense.

The most meaningful things begin where words end.

Chapter I · The Word

Gibberish: the art of emptying

Legend says the word comes from Jabbar, a Sufi mystic. He asked his seekers to sit and speak pure nonsense: every worry, every half-thought, every word that had been waiting inside them.

They spoke until there was nothing left to say. In that emptiness, something rare appeared: silence. Not the silence of a quiet room, but the silence of a quiet mind.

We took his name for our house because that’s what a true fragrance does. It cuts through the noise and brings you back to yourself.

Chapter II · Our Mark

The one who laughs in stillness

Our mark is the laughing Buddha, seated in meditation and glowing from within.

He reminds us that peace isn’t serious. Stillness and laughter aren’t opposites; they’re the same freedom. Someone who has let go of the noise can laugh from the belly, sit in silence and feel full.

That’s the feeling we want each bottle to carry: lightness with depth, joy with calm.

We don’t sell perfume. We sell the moment after the noise.

Chapter III · The Way of Attar

Some things cannot be hurried

For centuries, perfumers have captured flowers, woods and resins in oil, slowly, the way they have always done it. We honour that patience.

I

Gather

Petals, woods and resins, each taken at its most fragrant moment.

II

Distil

Slow heat and patient hands. The soul of the plant passes into oil.

III

Rest

Aged until every sharp edge softens into harmony.

IV

Become

On your skin it changes again, until it smells like no one but you.

Chapter IV · Our Promise

If we wouldn’t wear it, we won’t sell it.

“Pehle humne pehna, phir aapke liye chuna.”
First we wore it. Then we chose it for you.

Every fragrance in our collection, from our own attars to the houses we admire, has been worn by us, on our own skin, through a full day.

If it fades, we don’t list it. If it doesn’t move us, we don’t list it. What’s left is a small collection we’d give to people we love.
